A concept for discussion

Skilled volunteering that works for everyone involved

Canadian nonprofits need professional skills they can't afford. Professionals want to give them. What's missing is a system that makes it easy for four partners at once: nonprofits, volunteers, companies and foundations.

How it works

One project, five steps

Scope

A nonprofit describes what it needs. A template turns that into a clear project.

Match

The nonprofit chooses from a short list of volunteers with the right skills.

Onboard

The volunteer gets a briefing before the first meeting.

Deliver

Short check-ins keep the work moving and on time.

Measure

A closing report, then a check three months later to see if the work stuck.

AI does the preparation, the reminders and the reporting, so people spend their time on the work itself. A typical project: an AI use policy for a 12-person charity, 20 volunteer hours over four weeks.

Community

People stay because of other people

For volunteers

A circle of professionals doing the same kind of work, who share what they learned after each project.

For nonprofits

A peer group of leaders facing the same problem, who compare what worked after the volunteer left.

Finished work goes back into the library as an example, with consent, so the next nonprofit starts further ahead.
